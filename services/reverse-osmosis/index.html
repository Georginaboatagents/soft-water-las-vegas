Steps of Purification

1. Pre-Filters

"Melt-Blown" Pre-filter for fine filtration before our carbon block.

Carbon Block Filter uses coconut shell carbon exclusively for best results. Chlorine taste and odor reduction with superior sediment reduction.

2. Membrane

The Pentair Membrane removes most unwanted inorganics (about 98%) and most heavy metals. Any dissolved solids too small to be removed are sent to the drain.

3. Tanks

After passing through the pre-filters and membrane, the filtered water is kept in the storage tank. We offer different size tanks to fit your needs including one, two, three, and ten gallons.

4. Post-Filter

Pure water stored in the storage tank is now polished when you call for water at the faucet. Virtually anything left in the water is removed by the post-filter.

The Alkalizer Filter

The Alkalizer Filter is the newest creation from Ultima Water Products. It is the final filter before water travels to the faucet and it adds the healthy minerals back to your drinking water.

The Alkalizer filter has a proprietary blend developed by water treatment experts with over 75 years of combined experience. It was developed to address the growing health concerns associated with RO water.

Components of the Alkalizer Filter

  • Activated Carbon: gives water the final polish for great taste.
  • KDF 55: offers extra protection against bacteria and viruses.
  • Calcite: brings RO water PH back to equilibrium.
  • Mineral Blend: gives you the healthy minerals back the RO removes with special blend.
