The problem in every Las Vegas home

Our tap water is about 16 grains per gallon. "Very hard" starts at 10.5.

That mineral load comes out of every faucet, shower and appliance in your house, every day. It is why your glasses spot, your shower door clouds over and your water heater works harder each year.
